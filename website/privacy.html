Privacy policy

Draft. This policy must be reviewed by our legal advisers before the apps are published. Items marked [TBD] are not decided yet.

What we collect

  • Account: your phone number (used for sign-in with a one-time code), and your name and optional email.
  • Rides: pickup and drop locations, fares, ride status, ratings and comments, and support messages.
  • Location: captains share their location while online and during rides, so riders can see their captain and so we can match nearby requests. Customers share their location to set pickup points.
  • Captains: licence details, identity and vehicle documents you upload for verification, vehicle details, and plan and payment records.
  • Payments: payment status and amounts. Card and bank details are handled by our payment provider, not stored by us.

How we use it

To run rides, match captains with riders, calculate fares, verify captains, process payments, handle support requests, and keep the service safe.

Who we share it with

Riders and captains see each other's first name, vehicle, and live location while a ride is in progress. We share data with our payment provider, our SMS provider, and our hosting provider, only as needed to run the service.
