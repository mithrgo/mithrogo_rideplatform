The service

MithrGo connects riders with independent bike and auto captains in our launch city. We provide the app and the platform. Captains are independent operators, not employees of MithrGo.

Booking and fares

The fare shown before you book is the fare you pay for that ride. It is calculated by our system from the route and current rates.

Payments

You can pay online through the app or, where the ride is booked as a cash ride, in cash to your captain.

Cancellation

You can cancel a ride from the app until it starts. [TBD] Any cancellation charges.

Captains

Captains must complete verification and choose a plan before going online. A captain on the daily plan pays a fixed daily fee. A captain on the ride-share plan gives the platform the fare of one ride per day, as described in the app. Unpaid platform dues may stop a captain from going online until they are settled.

Conduct

Riders and captains must treat each other respectfully, follow the law, and provide accurate information. We may suspend accounts that break these rules.
